Operator manual

Production: Click manually or own generators. Upgrades multiply their output. Research: earns slowly after 100K lifetime Data and unlocks branching permanent ideas. Reboot: resets a run for Core Tokens. Singularity: is a deeper reset for reality-changing upgrades. Codes: redeem local one-time gifts. Offline: production is calculated from your last save. Saving: automatic localStorage saves rotate two backups; export regularly.

Everything is client-side and editable. This is an intentional single-player experience.
